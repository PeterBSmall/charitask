ChariTask Welcome Experience
Product Vision & Architecture
Purpose

The Welcome Experience is not simply an onboarding wizard. It is the user's first experience with ChariTask. Its purpose is to make users feel welcomed, confident, and excited before introducing them to the platform.

The onboarding should feel like a conversation, not a form.

Core Philosophy:

One Screen. One Question. One Decision.

Every screen asks only one question. Users progress naturally without ever feeling overwhelmed.

User Journey
Step 1 — Welcome

Question

What should we call you?

Purpose

Introduce ChariTask and establish a friendly, welcoming tone.

Emotion

"You belong here."

Step 2 — Organization

Question

What is your organization called?

Purpose

Begin defining the organization.

Emotion

"Every mission begins somewhere."

Step 3 — Team

Question

Are you setting up ChariTask for a team or for yourself?

Purpose

Understand the user's environment.

Emotion

"People are at the center."

Step 4 — Organization Structure

Question

How is your organization structured?

Options include:

Single Location
Multiple Locations
Department Based
Not Sure Yet

Emotion

"Clarity creates confidence."

Step 5 — Role

Question

What's your role?

Suggested choices:

Administrator
Owner / Founder
Manager
Team Leader
Coordinator
Project Lead
Operations
IT / Technical Lead
Other

Emotion

"Leadership is service."

Step 6 — Mission

Question

What are you hoping to accomplish?

Suggested choices:

Improve team coordination
Streamline operations
Increase productivity
Organize volunteers
Strengthen collaboration
Build a more organized workflow
Support an important initiative
Other

Emotion

"Technology should serve purpose."

Step 7 — Begin

Question

Which workspace would you like to set up first?

Examples:

Foundation
People
Scheduling
Resources
Communications
Other Workspaces

Emotion

"You're ready."

Overall Story

Instead of seven forms, the Welcome Experience tells a story.

Welcome
Organization
Team
Structure
Leadership
Mission
Begin

Every screen moves the user naturally toward their first workspace.

Visual Experience

The left panel should reinforce the emotional journey.

Each onboarding step has its own full-height image.

The images should be:

Bright
Friendly
Authentic
Modern
Diverse
Purpose-driven

Avoid:

Organization-specific branding
Logos
Habitat-specific imagery
Corporate stock-photo feel

The images should represent people working together to accomplish meaningful work.
