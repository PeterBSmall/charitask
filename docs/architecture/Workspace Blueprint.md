# Workspace Blueprint

## Purpose

A Workspace is not a page.

A Workspace is a mission-focused environment that assembles the people, guidance, tools, and insights needed to accomplish meaningful work.

---

# Every Workspace Answers One Question

🏛 Foundation
Who are we?

👥 People
Who helps us?

📋 Projects
What are we accomplishing together?

⚙️ Operations
How do we get work done?

🛍 Commerce
How do we sustain the mission?

🤝 Community
How do we engage supporters?

📈 Impact
What difference are we making?

---

# Workspace Structure

Every Workspace follows the same structure.

1. Workspace Hero

Introduces the workspace.

Shows:

• Welcome
• Mission
• Progress
• Next Step
• Quick Actions
• Workspace Health

---

2. Mission Guide

Acts as a mentor.

Shows:

• Learning
• Setup
• Recommendations
• Milestones
• Celebrations

---

3. Metrics

Displays the health of the workspace.

Each workspace chooses its own metrics.

---

4. Modules

Modules are tools.

Workspaces are destinations.

---

5. Activity

Shows what changed.

Recent work.

Achievements.

Updates.

---

6. Insights

AI recommendations.

Helpful suggestions.

Potential issues.

Opportunities.

# Design Principle

People don't come looking for software.

They come to accomplish meaningful work.

Every Workspace should provide the guidance,
relationships,
tools,
and insights needed to help them succeed.
