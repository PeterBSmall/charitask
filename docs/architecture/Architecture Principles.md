# Architecture Principles

These principles guide every architectural decision in ChariTask.

---

## Mission before software

Every architectural decision should ultimately help organizations advance
their mission.

Technology exists to serve people.

---

## Context before components

The platform should understand a person's context before deciding what
information, tools, or guidance to present.

---

## Decisions before navigation

People should not have to search for what matters.

The platform should guide them toward meaningful work.

---

## Workspaces provide context

Workspaces define where a person is operating.

They do not contain business logic.

---

## Modules provide capabilities

Modules expose functionality.

They should never decide when they are used.

The Decision Engine orchestrates them.

---

## The Decision Engine is the brain

The Decision Engine transforms context into action.

It reasons.

It prioritizes.

It explains.

It learns.

---

## Every recommendation should be explainable

If the platform recommends an action,
it should always be able to explain why.

---

## Learning improves decisions

Every outcome improves future recommendations.

The platform continuously becomes more helpful.

---

## Simplicity is intelligence

The best software removes complexity
instead of exposing it.
