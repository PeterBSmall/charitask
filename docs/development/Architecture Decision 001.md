# ADR-001

## Decision

ChariTask is architected around a Decision Engine rather than traditional screen-driven navigation.

## Reason

Mission-driven organizations need contextual guidance, not just software features.

The platform will understand organizational context before presenting tools or recommendations.

## Consequences

- Workspaces provide context.
- Modules provide capabilities.
- The Decision Engine orchestrates behavior.
- AI Mentor uses the same contextual understanding.
